Stage 1: Shell Awakening

Phase 1 (Free) — Awaken X in the terminal grid.

Objective

Write a Bash script that renders a 10×5 ASCII grid and places the player character X at position (0, 0). This is your first transcript — the starting state that all future stages build upon.

Concepts Covered

  • Variables and arithmetic in Bash
  • Nested for loops
  • String construction and echo output

Challenge: Draw the Grid

Fill in the script below so that it prints a 10-column × 5-row grid. Place X at row 0, column 0; all other cells should show ..

#!/bin/bash
GRID_W=10
GRID_H=5
PLAYER_X=0
PLAYER_Y=0

for ((row=0; row<GRID_H; row++)); do
    line=""
    for ((col=0; col<GRID_W; col++)); do
        if [[ $row -eq $PLAYER_Y && $col -eq $PLAYER_X ]]; then
            line+="X "
        else
            line+=". "
        fi
    done
    echo "$line"
done

Expected output:

X . . . . . . . . .
. . . . . . . . . .
. . . . . . . . . .
. . . . . . . . . .
. . . . . . . . . .
